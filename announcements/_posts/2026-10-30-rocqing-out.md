# Hello!

How do you know a program (like your kernel) works correctly?  The typical answer is tests, but you can't predict every case in your tests.  The solution is a proof assistant, a programming language where you can state and prove logical statements.  This week, Lyran will introduce us to one such proof assistant: Rocq.

Prove you exist from 4:30-6:00pm this Friday in GOL-1445!
